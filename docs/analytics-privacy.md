## How we measure the website

We run our own analytics on this website to understand which pages and episodes people find useful. We don't use Google Analytics, advertising trackers or any other outside service for this, and we don't sell or share the data.

**What we record.** When you open a page we record the page, the website that sent you here (if any), campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source), the kind of device based on your screen width, your browser and operating system names, your language and time zone, and your approximate location (country, region and city) looked up from your connection by our hosting provider. While you're on a page we record how long it's on your screen while you're active and how far you scroll. We also record actions like playing a video, clicking a link to another site, signing up for the newsletter or adding merch to your bag. For heatmaps we record where on the page people click and, on computers, where the mouse moves, as counts in a grid. We never record what you type into forms, passwords or payment details.

**What we don't store.** We never store your IP address or your full browser signature. To count visitors we combine them with a random value that changes every day and keep only the scrambled result. The random value is deleted every day, so these counts can't be linked from one day to the next or traced back to you.

**No cookies.** We don't set cookies for analytics. We keep two random IDs in your browser's own storage:

- a **visit ID** in session storage, which disappears when you close the tab and changes after 30 minutes without activity;
- a **browser ID** in local storage, kept for up to 13 months, so we can tell when someone comes back. If you sign up for our newsletter from this browser, we link your email address to this browser ID so we can see which episodes our subscribers enjoy.

**When we don't set a browser ID.** We don't set the browser ID, and we keep no history for your browser, if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, or if you're in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. Your visits still count in our totals through the daily method above.

**How long we keep it.** Individual records are deleted after about 13 months (400 days). We keep daily totals (for example, page views per page per day) after that.

**How to opt out.** Turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. To have the records for your browser ID deleted, email [hello@rabbleandrouse.com] and we'll remove them.

**Who can see it.** Only the [Rabble & Rouse] team, through a password-protected dashboard. The data is stored with our hosting and database providers ([Vercel] and [Neon]) in [the United States].
