About the episode

Power. Consent. Rope.

Katie sits down with professional dominatrix, EMT, and helicopter pilot Mistress Eustacia for a thoughtful conversation about what it really means to give up control, power exchange, consent, vulnerability, and what actually happens inside a professional dungeon.

Mistress Eustacia shares her philosophy on setting boundaries, protecting your peace, getting comfortable saying no, and recognizing all the places we surrender our power without realizing it, from relationships to social media. And yes, there is rope.
